Questions About the Work

Where was the leak seen, and where is its source assumed?

A stain shows where water arrived, not necessarily where it began. A guess that the upstairs bath is responsible may be reasonable, but it should travel as a guess.

  • Note how the moisture behaves

    Whether the moisture changes with use is the opening question for the respondent. Record which fixtures were running, whether the spot is wet or merely stained, and how it looks compared with an earlier day, and take dated photographs. Mention any water meter or bill reading you have noted.

  • Compare the suspected spot with a wider search

    Compare a repair at the suspected spot with a wider search along the route the water may have travelled. In your Innisfil home, ask the respondent which observations back the chosen area and which are carried over from someone else's theory.

  • Separate the checks from the first guess

    Ask for a written record of the non-invasive checks made, the openings that were needed and the evidence found at each one, listed apart from the original guess. Note whether drying or restoration is part of the scope. Ask whether the first guess about the source was borne out, ruled out or left open.
